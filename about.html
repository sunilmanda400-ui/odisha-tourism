About Odisha

History

Odisha has a rich and glorious history that spans over two millennia. In ancient times, it was known as Kalinga, a powerful kingdom famous for its maritime trade, valor, and cultural influence across Southeast Asia.

The historic Kalinga War (261 BCE) fought by Emperor Ashoka changed the course of Indian history. Witnessing the devastation of war, Ashoka embraced Buddhism and promoted peace and non-violence throughout his empire.

During the medieval period, Odisha flourished under the Eastern Ganga and Gajapati dynasties, which built architectural marvels like the Jagannath Temple, Konark Sun Temple, and Lingaraj Temple.

Culture

Odisha’s culture is a harmonious blend of classical traditions and tribal heritage. Religion, temples, rituals, music, and dance are deeply woven into everyday life.

Odissi, one of India’s oldest classical dance forms, originated in the temples of Odisha and is known for its graceful movements and expressive storytelling.

The state is home to more than 60 tribal communities, each preserving unique customs, languages, festivals, and art forms, making Odisha culturally diverse and vibrant.

Festivals

Festivals in Odisha are celebrated with devotion, joy, and grandeur, reflecting the spiritual and agricultural roots of the state.

  • Rath Yatra: The world-famous chariot festival of Lord Jagannath in Puri
  • Durga Puja: Celebrated with artistic pandals and cultural programs
  • Raja Parba: A unique festival honoring womanhood and Mother Earth
  • Konark Dance Festival: Classical dances performed near the Sun Temple
  • Nuakhai: Harvest festival of Western Odisha

Food

Odisha cuisine is simple, nutritious, and deeply influenced by temple traditions. Rice is the staple food, often served with lentils, vegetables, and mild spices.

One of the highlights of Odia cuisine is the Mahaprasad of the Jagannath Temple, prepared without onion or garlic and served to thousands of devotees daily.

  • Pakhala Bhata: Fermented rice served with curd and vegetables
  • Dalma: Lentils cooked with seasonal vegetables
  • Chuda Mix: Flattened rice mixed with spices and nuts
  • Chhena Poda: Famous baked cottage cheese dessert
  • Rasabali: Deep-fried chhena soaked in thick milk syrup

Art & Handicrafts

Odisha is globally recognized for its exquisite handicrafts, passed down through generations of skilled artisans.

These crafts not only reflect artistic excellence but also narrate stories from mythology, nature, and everyday life.

  • Pattachitra: Traditional cloth-based scroll paintings
  • Silver Filigree (Tarakasi): Intricate silver wire artwork from Cuttack
  • Stone & Wood Carving: Inspired by temple architecture
  • Applique Work: Colorful fabric art from Pipili
  • Handloom Textiles: Sambalpuri, Ikat, and Bomkai sarees
